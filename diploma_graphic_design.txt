DIPLOMA IN GRAPHIC DESIGN

SECTION A: PROGRAMME INFORMATION
--------------------------------
Programme Course: Diploma in Graphic Design
Programme Code: JPT/BPP(K) (A10126)
Programme Summary:
A 2.5-year diploma programme designed to develop creative and technical design skills. Students learn digital design, branding, multimedia tools, and build a professional portfolio for career or further studies.

Awarding Body: YES International College
Duration: 2.5 years (7 semesters)
Mode of Study: Full-time
Intake Months: January, May, September

Entry Requirements:
- SPM: Minimum 3 credits
- O-Level: Minimum 3 credits
- UEC qualification accepted

SECTION B: PROGRAMME STRUCTURE
--------------------------------
Total Credit Hours: 90

Main Subjects Overview:
- Foundations: Drawing, Colour Theory, Typography
- Digital Design: Image Manipulation, Graphic Design, Webpage Design, Desktop Publishing
- Advertising & Marketing: Advertising Principles, Marketing Fundamentals, Research
- Photography: Basic and Commercial Photography
- Specialized Design: Packaging, Print, Corporate Identity
- Professional Development: Presentation Skills, Seminar, Final Project
- MPU Compulsory Modules

Course Classification:
1. Major (Core)
2. Compulsory MPU Courses
