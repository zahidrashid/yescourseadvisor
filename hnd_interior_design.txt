HIGHER NATIONAL DIPLOMA (HND) IN INTERIOR DESIGN

SECTION A: PROGRAMME INFORMATION
--------------------------------
Programme Course: Higher National Diploma in Art & Design (Interior Design)
Programme Code: 603/0908/8

Programme Summary:
A 2-year UK-based qualification that provides essential interior design skills through a blend of creative concepts and practical application. The programme covers space planning, materials, lighting, furniture design, CAD, and sustainable design, preparing students for professional practice or further studies.

Awarding Body: Pearson BTEC (UK)
Duration: 2 Years
Mode of Study: Full-time
Intake Months: January, May, September

Entry Requirements:
- SPM / O-Level / UEC
- OR equivalent qualifications

SECTION B: PROGRAMME STRUCTURE
--------------------------------
Total Credit Hours: 240 credits

Main Subjects Overview:
- Interior Design Principles
- 3D Spatial Design
- Computer-Aided Design (CAD)
- Materials & Structures
- Professional Practice
- Visual Merchandising & Branding

Level 4 Units:
- Unit 1 – Professional Development
- Unit 2 – Contextual Studies
- Unit 3 – Individual Project
- Unit 4 – Techniques & Processes
- Unit 5 – 3D Practices
- Unit 6 – Materials & Structures
- Unit 7 – Computer-Aided Design (CAD)
- Unit 24 – Visual Merchandising

Level 5 Units:
- Unit 32 – Professional Practice
- Unit 33 – Applied Practice Collaborative Project
- Unit 34 – Advanced Interior Design Studies
- Unit 46 – Technical Drawing
- Unit 47 – Branding & Identity
- Unit 50 – Material Selection & Specification

SECTION C: STUDY MODE & ASSESSMENT
--------------------------------
Overall Study Mode:
- Lectures, tutorials, workshops, and project-based learning

Assessment:
- 100% coursework-based (projects, portfolios, reports, presentations)
- Turnitin submission required (low similarity expected)

Grading System:
- Pass (P)
- Merit (M)
- Distinction (D)

SECTION D: CAREER & PROGRESSION
--------------------------------
Career Pathways:
- Interior Designer
- Space Planner
- CAD Technician
- Furniture Designer
- Visual Merchandiser

Degree Progression:
- Bachelor of Interior Design (Hons)
- Bachelor of Interior Architecture
- Bachelor of Furniture Design or Sustainable Design
