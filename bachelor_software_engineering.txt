BACHELOR OF COMPUTER SCIENCE (HONOURS) IN SOFTWARE ENGINEERING

SECTION A: PROGRAMME INFORMATION
--------------------------------
Programme Course: Bachelor of Computer Science (Honours) in Software Engineering (in collaboration with New Era University College)
Programme Code: MQA/PA19086

Programme Summary:
A 3-year undergraduate programme offered by YES International College in collaboration with New Era University College. The programme equips students with strong foundations in software development, programming, system design, and quality assurance. It combines theoretical learning with practical projects and industrial training to prepare graduates for careers in the IT and software engineering fields.

Awarding Body: New Era University College (with Kolej Antarabangsa YES)
Duration: 3 Years
Mode of Study: Full-time (Semester-based)
Intake Months: January, May, September

Entry Requirements:
- Foundation / Matriculation: CGPA ≥ 2.00 + credit in Additional Mathematics
- STPM: Minimum Grade C (any 2 subjects) + credit in Additional Mathematics
- Relevant Diploma: CGPA ≥ 2.50 (or ≥ 2.00 subject to assessment)
- Other Science/Technology Diploma: CGPA ≥ 2.50 (subject to assessment)
- Candidates without Additional Mathematics may be admitted with equivalent qualification
- Preference given to applicants with computing-related background
- International: MUET Band 3 / CEFR Low B2
