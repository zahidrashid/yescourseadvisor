BACHELOR OF ARTS (HONOURS) IN VISUAL COMMUNICATION

SECTION A: PROGRAMME INFORMATION
--------------------------------
Programme Course: Bachelor of Arts (Honours) in Visual Communication (in collaboration with New Era University College)
Programme Code: MQA/PA19240

Programme Summary:
A 3-year undergraduate programme delivered in collaboration with New Era University College. The programme combines lectures, practical studio work, and industrial training to develop strong visual communication skills. Students are prepared for careers in creative industries such as multimedia design, branding, and digital media.

Awarding Body: New Era University College (with Kolej Antarabangsa YES)
Duration: 3 Years
Mode of Study: Full-time (Semester-based)
Intake Months: January, May, September

Entry Requirements:
- STPM: Minimum Grade C (any 2 subjects)
- STAM: Minimum Jayyid
- Foundation / Matriculation: CGPA ≥ 2.00
- Diploma: CGPA ≥ 2.00
- DKM/DLKM/DVM: CGPA ≥ 2.50 (or ≥ 2.00 with 2 years experience)
- Other equivalent qualifications
- Interview or portfolio required
- International: MUET Band 3 or equivalent
