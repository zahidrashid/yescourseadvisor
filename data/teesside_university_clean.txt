TEESSIDE UNIVERSITY (TOP-UP DEGREE PARTNERSHIP PROGRAMME)

SECTION A: PROGRAMME INFORMATION
--------------------------------
Programme Overview: Teesside University Top-Up Degrees (Malaysia Partnership Programme)

Programme Summary:
Teesside University, a modern UK institution based in Middlesbrough with an additional London campus, offers top-up degree programmes in Malaysia through collaboration with YES International College. Known for its strong industry focus and student experience, the university provides pathways for students to complete a full Bachelor's Honours degree after a diploma or HND qualification.

Awarding Body: Teesside University (UK)
Duration: Approximately 1 year (Top-Up Degree)
Mode of Study: Full-time or Flexible Online
Location: Malaysia (via partnership) / UK
