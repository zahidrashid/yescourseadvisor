YES AMJB Art Incubation Centre

Introduction
Established in 2024 as a collaboration between YES International College and the Aesthetics Museum of Johor Bahru (AMJB).
This centre is a hub for art, design, technology, and innovation. It focuses on nurturing talent, integrating AI into creative processes,
and promoting aesthetic living, cultural tourism, and creative industries. Located in Johor Bahru as a branch campus,
it honors cultural heritage while embracing future tech, including a planned Cultural & Art Heritage and Financial Development Research Centre.

Vision
Build a thriving aesthetics ecosystem with AI-driven creativity, technology integration, and future-ready skills.

Activities
Empower graduates with real-world skills, drive creative industries, and promote aesthetic appreciation.
