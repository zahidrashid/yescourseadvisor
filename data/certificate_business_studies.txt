CERTIFICATE IN BUSINESS STUDIES

SECTION A: PROGRAMME INFORMATION
--------------------------------
Programme Course: Certificate in Business Studies
Programme Code: MQA/PA18733

Programme Summary:
A 1.5-year certificate programme that provides foundational knowledge in business administration, management, and operations. The programme develops essential business skills for entry-level roles or further study.

Awarding Body: YES International College
Duration: 1.5 Years
Mode of Study: Full-time
Intake Months: January, May, September

Entry Requirements:
- SPM: Minimum 1 credit
- SKM Level 2 in related field
- Other recognised equivalent qualifications
