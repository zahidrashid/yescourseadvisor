DIPLOMA IN BUSINESS MANAGEMENT

SECTION A: PROGRAMME INFORMATION
--------------------------------
Programme Course: Diploma in Business Management
Programme Code: R3/0410/4/0027 MQA/A9846
Programme Summary:
2.5-year diploma designed to provide students with a strong foundation in business principles and practical managerial skills. Covers management, marketing, accounting, finance, HR, entrepreneurship, and business communication.

Awarding Body: YES International College
Duration: 2.5 years (7 semesters)
Mode of Study: Full-time
Intake Months: January, May, September

Entry Requirements:
- SPM: Minimum 3 credits
- OR equivalent qualifications
