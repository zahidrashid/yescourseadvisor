1. OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------
This document outlines the bursary-related information, including types of 
fees, payment requirements, payment methods, and important policies for 
students at YES International College.

All students are required to understand and comply with the payment 
guidelines to ensure smooth academic progression.

2. TYPES OF FEES
--------------------------------------------------
Students are required to pay the following categories of fees:

A. Registration / Application Fee
- One-time payment upon enrollment

B. Semester / Tuition Fees
- Payable at the beginning of each semester

C. Other Fees
- Resource Fee
- Late Payment Charges
- Retake Fee
- Reprint Exam Docket Slip Fee
- Practical Kit Fee
- Any additional administrative charges
【1-a9f58f】

--------------------------------------------------
3. PAYMENT POLICY
--------------------------------------------------
- All fees must be paid in FULL before the start of each semester
- Payment must be completed before the due date or commencement of classes

Consequences of Late Payment:
- Imposition of late payment charges
- Students may be barred from:
  • Attending classes
  • Sitting for examinations
