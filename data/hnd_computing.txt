HIGHER NATIONAL DIPLOMA (HND) IN COMPUTING

SECTION A: PROGRAMME INFORMATION
--------------------------------
Programme Course: Higher National Diploma in Computing
Programme Code: 603/7596/6

Programme Summary:
A 2-year UK-based qualification designed to equip students with industry-relevant computing skills. The programme covers programming, networking, database design, software development, and security, preparing graduates for careers in the IT industry.

Awarding Body: Pearson BTEC (UK)
Duration: 2 Years
Mode of Study: Full-time
Intake Months: January, May, September

Entry Requirements:
- SPM / O-Level: Minimum 3 credits (including Mathematics)
- UEC: Minimum 3 Bs
- OR equivalent qualification (STPM, Foundation, Diploma)
- International students: IELTS 5.5 or equivalent

SECTION B: PROGRAMME STRUCTURE
--------------------------------
Total Credit Hours: 240 credits

Main Subjects Overview:
- Programming
- Networking
- Database Design & Development
- Software Development Lifecycle
- Cybersecurity
- Project Management

Level 4 Units:
- Unit 01 – Programming
- Unit 02 – Networking
- Unit 03 – Professional Practice
- Unit 04 – Database Design and Development
- Unit 05 – Security
- Unit 06 – Planning a Computing Project
- Unit 07 – Software Development Lifecycle
- Unit 14 – Maths for Computing

Level 5 Units:
- Unit 08 – Maths for Computing (Advanced)
- Unit 13 – Computing Research Project
- Unit 14 – Business Process Support
- Unit 18 – Discrete Mathematics
- Unit 21 – Application Programming Interfaces
- Unit 26 – Big Data Analytics and Visualisation
- Unit 29 – Network Security
- Unit 32 – Information Security Management

SECTION C: STUDY MODE & ASSESSMENT
--------------------------------
Overall Study Mode:
- 100% assignment-based assessment

Assessment Components:
- Assignment reports
- Presentations
- System development / project files
- Viva sessions
- Turnitin & plagiarism reports (≤15%)

Grading System:
- Pass (P)
- Merit (M)
- Distinction (D)
