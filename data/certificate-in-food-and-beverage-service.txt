
CERTIFICATE IN FOOD AND BEVERAGE SERVICE

SECTION A: PROGRAMME INFORMATION
--------------------------------
Programme Course: Certificate in Food and Beverage Service
Programme Code: MQA/PA18688
Programme Summary: Certificate focusing on practical skills and industry knowledge in food service operations, customer service, hygiene, and restaurant procedures.
Awarding Body: YES International College
Duration: 1.5 years (4 semesters + internship)
Mode of Study: Full-time
Intake Months: January, May, September
Entry Requirements:
- SPM: Minimum 1 credit in any subject OR equivalent
- OR Sijil Kemahiran Malaysia (SKM) Level 2 or equivalent
