DIPLOMA IN EARLY CHILDHOOD EDUCATION

SECTION A: PROGRAMME INFORMATION
--------------------------------
Programme Course: Diploma in Early Childhood Education
Programme Code: MQA/PA18657

Programme Summary:
A 2.5-year diploma programme focusing on early childhood development, pedagogy, curriculum planning, and professional practice. The programme prepares students to work with young children in educational and care settings.

Awarding Body: YES International College
Duration: 2 years 4 months (6 semesters + practicum)
Mode of Study: Full-time
Intake Months: January, May, September

Entry Requirements:
- SPM: Minimum 3 credits
- STPM: Minimum Grade C
- STAM: Minimum Maqbul
- Certificate (Level 3 MQF, CGPA ≥ 2.00)
- SKM/SVM Level 3 (Early Childhood field)
- Other equivalent qualifications
- International: MUET or CEFR Low B1
