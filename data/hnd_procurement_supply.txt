HIGHER NATIONAL DIPLOMA (HND) IN PROCUREMENT AND SUPPLY MANAGEMENT

SECTION A: PROGRAMME INFORMATION
--------------------------------
Programme Course: Pearson BTEC Level 5 Higher National Diploma in Business (Procurement and Supply Management)
Programme Code:
- Pearson BTEC Level 4: 603/6837/8
- Pearson BTEC Level 5: 603/6838/X

Programme Summary:
A 2-year UK-based assignment-driven programme focusing on business, procurement, and supply chain management. It emphasizes applied learning through real-world case studies, projects, and assignments, enabling students to develop analytical, decision-making, and problem-solving skills relevant to procurement and supply chain environments.

Awarding Body: Pearson BTEC (UK)
Duration: 2 Years
Mode of Study: Full-time
Intake Months: January, May, September

Entry Requirements:
- SPM: Minimum 3 credits
- OR equivalent qualifications

SECTION B: PROGRAMME STRUCTURE
