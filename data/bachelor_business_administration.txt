BACHELOR OF ARTS (HONOURS) IN BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

SECTION A: PROGRAMME INFORMATION
--------------------------------
Programme Course: Bachelor of Arts (Honours) in Business Administration (in collaboration with New Era University College)
Programme Code: MQA/PA19085

Programme Summary:
A 3-year undergraduate programme offered by YES International College in collaboration with New Era University College. The programme provides a comprehensive foundation in business disciplines including management, marketing, finance, human resources, and operations. Delivered through lectures, tutorials, and industrial training, it prepares students for professional careers and further studies in business.

Awarding Body: New Era University College (with Kolej Antarabangsa YES)
Duration: 3 Years
Mode of Study: Full-time (Semester-based)
Intake Months: January, May, September

Entry Requirements:
- STPM: Minimum Grade C (any 2 subjects)
- STAM: Minimum Jayyid
- Foundation/Matriculation: CGPA ≥ 2.00
- Diploma/Advanced Diploma: CGPA ≥ 2.00
- DKM/DLKM/DVM (with approval or experience where required)
- Other recognised equivalent qualifications
- Pass in Mathematics and English (SPM level)
- International: MUET Band 3 / CEFR Low B2
