CERTIFICATE IN HOTEL OPERATION

SECTION A: PROGRAMME INFORMATION
--------------------------------
Programme Course: Certificate in Hotel Operation
Programme Code: MQA/PA (18732)
Programme Summary: 1.8-year certificate programme designed to equip students with the ability to adapt to the dynamic hospitality environment.
Awarding Body: Yes International College
Duration: 1.8 years (4 semesters + internship)
Mode of Study: Full-time
Intake Months: January, May, September
Entry Requirements:
- SPM: Minimum 1 credit in any subject or equivalent
- OR Sijil Kemahiran Malaysia (SKM) Level 2 or equivalent

SECTION B: PROGRAMME STRUCTURE
--------------------------------
Total Credit Hours: 60

Main Subjects Overview:
- Practical skills in food & beverage operations
- Rooms division operations knowledge
- Managerial skills for hospitality careers
- Global and local employability readiness

Course Classification:
1. Compulsory
2. Major (Core)

Year 1 Subjects:
- MPU1153 / MPU1133 – Penghayatan Etika dan Peradaban / Bahasa Melayu Komunikasi I
- ENG1013 – English
- CHO1103 – Fundamental of Food Studies
- CHO1113 – Hotel Management and Operations
- CHO1143 – Introduction to Hospitality Industry
- MPU1233 / MPU1213 – Thinking Skills / Bahasa Kebangsaan A
- CHO2073 – Food and Beverage Service Skills
- CHO2183 – Food Hygiene and Sanitation
- CHO2233 – Computer Applications
- CHO2173 – Basic Accounting
- MPU1333 – Kursus Integriti dan Anti Rasuah
- CHO3043 – Customer Service
- CBS214 – Entrepreneurship

Year 2 Subjects:
- CHO4033 – Front Office Operations
- CHO4023 – Housekeeping Operations
- CHO4113 – Room Service
- CHO4153 – Innkeeper's Law

Industrial Training:
- Semester 5
- 6 months compulsory internship
