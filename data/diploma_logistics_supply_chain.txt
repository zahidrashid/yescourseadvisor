DIPLOMA IN LOGISTICS AND SUPPLY CHAIN MANAGEMENT

SECTION A: PROGRAMME INFORMATION
--------------------------------
Programme Course: Diploma in Logistics and Supply Chain Management
Programme Code: (N/345/4/1162)(08/27) (MQA/FA/13806)
Programme Summary:
A 3-year diploma programme that equips students with theoretical and practical skills in logistics, warehousing, inventory control, and supply chain coordination to improve business operations efficiency.

Awarding Body: YES International College
Duration: 3 years (6 semesters + internship)
Mode of Study: Full-time
Intake Months: January, May, September

Entry Requirements:
Local:
- SPM with at least 3 credits
- SKM Level 3 in related field
- Certificate (Level 3 MQF, CGPA ≥ 2.00)
- STPM (min Grade C)
- STAM (min Maqbul)
- Other equivalent qualifications

International:
- MUET Band 3 or CEFR High B1 or equivalent
