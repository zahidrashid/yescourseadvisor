DIPLOMA IN PROPERTY MANAGEMENT (UTM COLLABORATION)

SECTION A: PROGRAMME INFORMATION
--------------------------------
Programme Course: Diploma Pengurusan Harta Tanah (UTM Collaboration)
Programme Code: MQA/PA 16690
Programme Summary:
A 3-year diploma programme delivered in collaboration with Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM). It provides knowledge in property management, valuation, and real estate practices, covering law, valuation, building technology, and property management skills.

Awarding Body: Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM) in collaboration with YES International College
Duration: 3 years (6 semesters + internship)
Mode of Study: Full-time
Intake Months: January, May, September

Entry Requirements:
- SPM: Minimum 3 credits including Mathematics
- OR equivalent qualifications

SECTION B: PROGRAMME STRUCTURE
--------------------------------
Total Credit Hours: 90

Main Subjects Overview:
- Property Valuation & Finance
- Property Law & Legal Framework
- Building Technology & Services
- Property Management & Marketing
- Real Estate Practices

Course Classification:
1. Compulsory
2. Major (Core)
3. Elective (Core)
4. Minor

Year 2 Subjects:

Year 3 Subjects:
- Industrial Training (Final)

Industrial Training:
