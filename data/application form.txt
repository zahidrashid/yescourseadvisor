--------------------------------------------------

Declaration:
- Read and agree to all terms and conditions

- Agree to institutional rules and policies

Signature Requirement:
- Applicant must sign the declaration
- Applicants under 21 require parent/guardian signature

Refund Policy:
- Registration fee: Non-refundable
- Tuition Fee Refund:
