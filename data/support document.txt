==================================================
YES INTERNATIONAL COLLEGE
SUPPORTING DOCUMENTS – APPLICATION GUIDE
==================================================

1. OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
To successfully apply for admission, applicants must submit a completed 
application form together with all required supporting documents.

Requirement:
- All documents listed are mandatory
- Documents are used for verification, eligibility assessment, and compliance

Note:
Incomplete submissions may delay processing or result in rejection【1-ee4514】

--------------------------------------------------
2. APPLICATION FEE PAYMENT PROOF
--------------------------------------------------
Document Required:
- Copy of bank transfer receipt or transaction confirmation

Details:
- Payment must be made to YES Academy Sdn. Bhd.
- Must clearly show:
  • Payment amount
  • Date of transaction
  • Correct recipient account

Purpose:
- Confirms that the non-refundable application fee has been paid
【1-ee4514】

--------------------------------------------------
3. IDENTIFICATION DOCUMENTS
--------------------------------------------------
Document Required:
- Photocopy of IC (for Malaysian students)
- Photocopy of passport (for international students)

For International Students:
- Include:
  • Personal information page
  • Visa pages (if applicable)
  • Entry stamp pages

Purpose:
- Identity verification
- Immigration compliance
【1-ee4514】

--------------------------------------------------
4. PASSPORT-SIZED PHOTOGRAPHS
--------------------------------------------------
Document Required:
- 2 copies of recent passport-sized photos

Specifications:
- Size: 50mm (height) × 35mm (width)
- Background: Plain (white preferred)

Usage:
- Student ID card
- Official records
- Administrative purposes
【1-ee4514】

--------------------------------------------------
5. ACADEMIC DOCUMENTS
--------------------------------------------------
Document Required:
- Copies of high school certificates
- Copies of academic transcripts

Examples:
- SPM / O-Levels / IGCSE or equivalent

Requirements:
- Must show subjects and grades
- Must be clear and legible

For International Documents:
- Must include official translation if not in English or Malay

Purpose:
- Assess academic eligibility for selected programme【1-ee4514】

--------------------------------------------------
6. MEDICAL CHECKUP REPORT
--------------------------------------------------
Document Required:
- Copy of medical checkup results

Requirements:
- Conducted by registered clinic or hospital
- Should include:
  • General health assessment
  • Chest X-ray (e.g., tuberculosis screening)
  • Standard medical declaration

Importance:
- Required for student visa processing (especially international students)
- Must comply with EMGS regulations
【1-ee4514】

--------------------------------------------------
7. SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------
Applicants must:
- Submit all documents together with the completed application form
- Ensure all copies are:
  • Clear and legible
  • Certified if required

Additional Requirements:
- Include declaration signatures
- Parent/guardian signature required for applicants under 21 years old

--------------------------------------------------
8. IMPORTANT NOTES
--------------------------------------------------
- Always keep original documents for verification
- Originals may be requested during:
  • Registration
  • Orientation

Recommendation:
- Double-check all documents before submission
- Ensure no missing items

Outcome:
- Complete submissions ensure faster and smoother processing
【1-ee4514】
