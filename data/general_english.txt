GENERAL ENGLISH PROGRAMME

SECTION A: PROGRAMME INFORMATION
--------------------------------
Programme Course: General English
Programme Code: (Not specified)
Programme Summary:
The General English Program is a flexible course designed to improve overall English communication skills at various proficiency levels. The program ranges from 1 month up to 12 months and focuses on speaking, listening, reading, writing, grammar, and vocabulary. Learners are placed according to their level and progress at their own pace. It is suitable for beginners to advanced learners for daily communication, academic preparation, and professional use.

Awarding Body: YES International College
Duration: 1 Month – 12 Months
Mode of Study: Full-time
Intake Months: Every Month

Entry Requirements:
- No formal requirements
- Open to anyone interested in learning English

SECTION B: PROGRAMME STRUCTURE
--------------------------------
Total Credit Hours: Nil

Main Subjects Overview:
- Speaking & Pronunciation – Build fluency and confidence in communication
- Listening Skills – Understand conversations, presentations, and accents
- Reading Skills – Improve comprehension of texts and materials
- Writing Skills – Develop clear and accurate writing
- Grammar – Learn and apply correct structures
- Vocabulary Development – Expand word usage for different contexts
- Functional English – Real-life communication (travel, work, social)
- Phonetics – Understanding speech sounds and pronunciation systems (IPA)
