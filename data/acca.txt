ASSOCIATION OF CHARTERED CERTIFIED ACCOUNTANTS (ACCA)

SECTION A: PROGRAMME INFORMATION
--------------------------------
Programme Course: Association of Chartered Certified Accountants (ACCA)
Programme Code: MQA/FA8401

Programme Summary:
The ACCA is a globally recognised professional qualification in accounting and finance. It equips students with advanced knowledge and practical skills in financial management, auditing, taxation, and strategic business leadership, preparing them for professional careers in the accounting and finance industry.

Awarding Body: Association of Chartered Certified Accountants (ACCA)
Duration: 3 Years (6 semesters)
Mode of Study: Full-time / Part-time / Online
Intake Months: January, July

Entry Requirements:
- SPM: Minimum 3 credits including English and Mathematics
- OR equivalent qualifications

SECTION B: PROGRAMME STRUCTURE
--------------------------------
Main Subjects Overview:
- Accounting
- Finance
- Audit and Assurance
- Taxation
- Business Law
- Management and Strategy
- Ethics and Professional Skills

Year 1 Subjects:
- Business and Technology (BT)
- Financial Accounting (FA)
- Management Accounting (MA)
- MPU Subjects (for international students only)

Year 2 Subjects:
- Corporate and Business Law (LW)
- Taxation (TX)
- Financial Reporting (FR)
- Performance Management (PM)
- Financial Management (FM)
- Audit and Assurance (AA)

Year 3 Subjects:
Essentials:
- Strategic Business Reporting (SBR)
- Strategic Business Leader (SBL)

Options (choose any 2):
- Advanced Financial Management (AFM)
- Advanced Audit and Assurance (AAA)
- Advanced Taxation (ATX)
- Advanced Performance Management (APM)
