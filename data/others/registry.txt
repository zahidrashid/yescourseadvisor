YES International College Registry Department Overview

The Registry Department at YES International College is responsible for managing student administrative services, from admission to graduation. It is headed by Ms Norhanim Mohsin and supports both local and international students.

Department Leadership
Head: Ms Norhanim Mohsin

Main Intakes/Semesters
January – Long semester (14 weeks)
May – Long semester (14 weeks)
September – Short semester (7 weeks)

Services for Students

1. Admission and Record
- Maintains student documents
- Issues confirmation, bank, and completion letters
- Provides transcripts and certificates
- Tracks student status (withdrawal, deferment, etc.)
- Issues student ID cards

2. PTPTN Application (Local Students Only)
- Assists with loan documents
- Helps with SSPN account and PIN
- Guides online application and approval process

3. Student Services (International Students)
- Handles visa endorsement and renewal
- Manages insurance and passport processing
- Monitors visa expiry
- Assists with visa issues and Check Out Memo

4. Student Portal
- Allows updates of personal details
- Provides access to payments and academic results
