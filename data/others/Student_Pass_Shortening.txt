Student Pass Shortening/Cancellation Guide for International Students in
Malaysia

This guide outlines the process for shortening or cancelling your
student pass (also known as Shortening of Student Pass Application or
STSP), typically required when completing studies, withdrawing,
transferring institutions, or leaving Malaysia. The process is handled
through your college’s international/visa office (e.g., meet admin team
like Ms. Zaty and Ms. Sabreena) and EMGS/Immigration Department. It
usually takes up to 14 working days for processing, but plan for more
time to avoid issues.

Important Notes:

-   Start the process at least 1 month before your flight departure date
    or student pass expiry (whichever is sooner) to allow for submission
    and processing.
-   The flight ticket must be for a date at least 14 working days after
    submission to give time for approval.
-   If returning to your home country, provide a ticket there. If going
    to another country, you must provide proof of a valid visa or
    resident pass for that country.
-   You must be physically present in Malaysia to submit your original
    passport.
-   Fees: Approximately RM53 (EMGS processing fee), payable via bank
    transfer (e.g., to EMGS Escrow Account).
-   Overstay after cancellation can lead to fines (RM30/day initially).
    Do not leave Malaysia without proper cancellation, as it may result
    in blacklisting or other penalties.
-   If you have any issues (e.g., missing documents), contact
    Ms. Sabreena or Ms. Zaty for assistance.
-   This is a general guide based on standard EMGS procedures. Confirm
    with your college’s international office for specifics, as
    requirements may vary (e.g., UCSI, UTAR, APU).

Required Documents

Gather these based on your situation. Bring originals and photocopies
where specified.

-   Original passport (including any renewed passport).
-   2 photocopies of the passport personal information page and the
    latest student pass page (one set for EMGS submission, one for your
    records or undertaking letter).
-   Proof of completion/withdrawal/transfer:
    -   Online Completion/Withdrawal Form (approved by your
        faculty/registrar; for completion, withdrawal, or deferral).
    -   New Letter of Acceptance (LOA) if progressing/changing programs
        within the same institution.
    -   Offer letter from the new institution if transferring.
    -   Supporting documents if applying for another visa (e.g., work
        permit, spouse visa: job offer, marriage certificate, etc.).
-   Flight ticket:
    -   Printed e-ticket to your home country (direct route preferred;
        transit must be within 24 hours).
    -   If not returning home, photocopy of valid visa or resident pass
        for the destination country.
-   Payment receipt for EMGS fee.
-   Explanation letter (if required by your college, stating reasons for
    shortening/cancellation).
-   Any college-specific forms (e.g., Format A or undertaking letter).

Step-by-Step Process

-   Assess Your Situation and Prepare Documents Determine why you’re
    shortening/cancelling (e.g., completing studies, withdrawing,
    transferring, or leaving Malaysia).
    -   Ensure you meet prerequisites (e.g., cleared all fees, 80%
        attendance if applicable, GPA 2.0+ for transcripts).
    -   Book a flight ticket with a departure date at least 14 working
        days after planned submission (to allow processing time). The
        ticket should be within 1 month for timely submission-start the
        process within 1 month before your ticket date.
    -   If not returning to your home country, obtain and photocopy a
        valid visa/resident pass for the destination.
    -   Prepare all required documents listed above. Pay the EMGS fee
        and keep the receipt.
-   Contact College Admin Team Visit your college in person to initiate
    the process. Meet with the admin team (e.g., Ms. Zaty and
    Ms. Sabreena at the international/visa office).
    -   Explain your reason for shortening/cancellation.
    -   They will provide any necessary forms (e.g.,
        Completion/Withdrawal Form) and guide you on specifics.
    -   Submit your documents at least 1 month before your student pass
        expiry or flight date. If problems arise (e.g., invalid ticket),
        discuss with Ms. Sabreena or Ms. Zaty for alternatives.
-   Submit Application Hand over your original passport and all
    documents to the admin team.
    -   The college will submit the application to EMGS or Immigration
        on your behalf (online or in-person).
    -   Processing time: Up to 14 working days (may vary; follow up via
        your college or EMGS portal at
        https://visa.educationmalaysia.gov.my/).
    -   During this period, you cannot travel overseas or to East
        Malaysia, as your passport is surrendered.
-   Pay Fees and Await Approval Confirm payment of the RM53 EMGS fee
    (already done in Step 1).
    -   Monitor progress through your college admin.
    -   Once approved, the student pass will be shortened/cancelled and
        endorsed in your passport (e.g., new expiry date matching your
        departure).
-   Collect Passport and Depart Collect your passport from the college
    admin or Immigration (as advised) once notified (typically within 1
    week of approval).
    -   Verify the shortened pass details.
    -   Depart Malaysia on or before the new expiry date using your
        booked flight.
    -   If graduating, your academic certificate/transcript may only be
        released after cancellation.
-   Follow Up on Any Issues If delays occur or you need extensions
    (e.g., via Special Pass), contact Ms. Sabreena or Ms. Zaty
    immediately.
    -   For overstay or other complications, you may need to pay fines
        (RM30 per day initially) and attend Immigration.
    -   Ensure all is resolved before leaving to avoid future entry
        issues.

Note: Refer to official EMGS guidelines for the latest updates: EMGS
Student Pass Guidelines. If transferring institutions, complete
shortening before applying for a new pass. Always consult your college
for personalized advice.
