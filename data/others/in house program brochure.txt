IN-HOUSE CORPORATE TRAINING PROGRAM
"Know Yourself, Know Others"
==================================================

1. PROGRAM OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------
Program Type        : 2-Day Corporate Training (HRDC Claimable)
Focus              : Psychology-Based Experiential Learning

Objective:
- Strengthen team communication
- Enhance emotional intelligence (EQ)
- Improve collaboration and teamwork
- Build self-awareness among participants

Target Audience:
- Cross-functional teams
- Managers and supervisors
- HR professionals
- Leadership groups

--------------------------------------------------
2. INTERNATIONAL COLLABORATION
--------------------------------------------------
- Program co-developed with GoodHabits Hong Kong
- Trainers are TOT-certified (Train-the-Trainer)
- Based on experiential learning principles

Learning Methodology:
- Kolb’s Experiential Learning Cycle
- HKU-informed learning approach
- Practical, real-world applications

--------------------------------------------------
3. KEY LEARNING COMPONENTS
--------------------------------------------------
- Self-awareness and understanding others
- Team communication techniques
- Emotional intelligence (EQ) skills
- Feedback and reflection
- Collaboration and problem-solving

--------------------------------------------------
4. SAMPLE ACTIVITIES
--------------------------------------------------
- Blind Spot Challenge
  • Focus: Communication and trust building

- Magic Hat Feedback
  • Focus: Constructive feedback and reflection

- Guided Reflection & Action Planning
  • Focus: Personal improvement and goal setting

- Domino Collaboration Task
  • Focus: Team problem-solving and coordination

- EQ Scenarios
  • Focus: Real-world emotional intelligence application

- DISC Profiling Session
  • Focus: Personal behavior and personality insights
