What Does GEFT Mean
GEFT stands for Global Education Foundation — an innovative online platform that beautifully combines education, investment, and social good!
You can start with a small amount (as low as $10), support real colleges and learning opportunities around the world, and potentially grow your wealth through:
GEFTokens (their special token that may increase in value) Dividend rewards from educational projects Exciting affiliate bonuses when you invite friends to join the journey
Plus, the platform partners with great institutions like YES International College in Kuala Lumpur, so you can explore and even help others enroll in popular diploma programs (Business Management, Information Systems, Logistics & Supply Chain, Graphic Design, and more) while earning rewards along the way. It’s designed to be simple, modern, crypto-friendly (using secure USDT), and open to people from many countries — including Malaysia. Many members love the idea of earning while making a positive impact on education!
Step-by-Step: How to Register on GEFT (Super Easy & Quick!)
    1. Visit the Official Website
    • Open your browser and go to → https://geft.io/ (Always type it yourself for safety — the homepage looks clean and welcoming.)
    1. Click the Join / Register Button
    • Look for a bright button like “Start with $10”, “Join Now”, or “Register” — usually right on the front page or in the top menu.
    • Click it and you’ll be taken to the sign-up form — it’s fast and straightforward!
    1. Fill in Your Personal Details The form is simple and only asks for the essentials:
    • User ID: Choose something fun and unique that represents you (e.g., your nickname, initials + numbers, etc.). It’s compulsory and will be how others see you in the system.
    • Full Name: Enter your real full name.
    • Email: Use an active email you check often (this is where verification goes).
    • Country: Select Malaysia (or your country).0
    • *Mobile No.: Enter your number with country code (e.g., +60 for Malaysia).
All starred () fields are required — just fill them honestly.
    1. Add the Referral User ID (Important & Easy) In the Referral Details section, you will see:
    • Referral User ID* → Keep it as YES (this is the default or recommended referral).
    • It’s already filled as YES in most cases — don’t change it unless someone specifically gave you a different code.
    • This helps connect you to the community and may give you extra welcome benefits!
    1. Agree to the Terms Tick the box: “I read and agree to the Terms of Conditions & Privacy Policy” (It’s standard — just make sure you understand the basics before ticking.)
    2. Hit Register Click the big Register button at the bottom. You’re done! 🎉
    • You’ll usually get a verification email or SMS code right away.
    • Enter the code to confirm your account.
    • Then log in at https://app.geft.io/ with your email + password.
    1. First Steps After Registering
    • Log in → You’ll land on a beautiful dashboard (gold/olive theme) with your welcome message.
    • Check your balances (Bonus, USDT, GEFTokens) — they start at zero, which is normal.
    • Explore the YES College products section — see all the exciting diploma options!
    • If you want to start investing or buying packages, go to the Deposit-Crypto area and follow the safe instructions there.
