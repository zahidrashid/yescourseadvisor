SECTION 5: NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  A. FULL-TIME PROGRAM
  ---------------------

  * Loan amount level is determined based on the following eligibility:

  No.   Loan Amount             Eligibility
  ---   -----------             -----------
  1.    Maximum                 Student or student's guardian is listed as a
                                recipient of Sumbangan Tunai Rahmah (STR)

  2.    75% of maximum loan     Family income not exceeding RM8,000.00
                                (not in STR data)

  3.    50% of maximum loan     Family income exceeding RM8,000.00
                                (not in STR data)

  * The maximum loan amount for the Full-Time Diploma program has been increased
    from RM6,800.00 to RM8,000.00 per year for private institutions (IPTS) that
    agree to provide takaful savings for B40 Full-Time Diploma students.

  * The 75% and 50% loan amounts remain at existing amounts, which are 75% and
    50% of RM6,800.00 per year.


  B. PART-TIME PROGRAM
  ---------------------

  i.  The actual education loan amount is determined based solely on the tuition
      fees and does not exceed the specified ceiling amount.

  ii. Part-time loans for private institutions (IPTS) are provided only for:
        - Universiti Terbuka Malaysia
        - Universiti Terbuka Wawasan
        - Universiti Tun Abdul Razak (UniRazak)
        - Asia e University
        - UNITAR


  C. LOAN DISBURSEMENT
  ---------------------

  i.  The first loan disbursement is made after the student returns the complete
      agreement documents to PTPTN.

  ii. Loan disbursements for subsequent semesters are made based on the
      following conditions:

        1. Still continuing studies at the approved level, major, and
           institution;

        2. Passed the examination in the previous semester with a Grade Point
           Average (PNG) of 2.0 and above; and

        3. Not receiving any other sponsorship.

  iii. PTPTN is NOT involved in the deduction of institution tuition fees.
       The loan amount will be credited directly to the student's account
       according to the semester, subject to confirmation provided by the
       institution.

       Loan payment checks can be made via:
         - Loan statement
         - Borrower's bank account statement

       Any inquiries involving tuition fee deductions should be referred to
       the respective institution.
