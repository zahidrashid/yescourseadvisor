PUBLIC TRAINING PROGRAM
"Know Yourself, Know Others"

1. PROGRAM OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------
Program Type        : Public Training (Open Enrollment)
Duration            : 2 Days
Certification       : HRDC Claimable

Description:
This public training program is designed for individuals and small teams seeking 
personal and professional growth. It focuses on improving self-awareness, emotional 
intelligence, communication skills, and interpersonal effectiveness.

Participants engage in hands-on activities, guided reflection, and real-world 
scenarios to build confidence and empathy for both personal and workplace interactions.
