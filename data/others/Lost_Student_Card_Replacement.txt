Lost Student Card Replacement Policy In the event that a student loses
their student card, a replacement can be issued subject to the following
conditions:

Students are required to pay a replacement fee of RM50 for each lost
card. The replacement card will only be processed after payment has been
made in full. Students must submit a replacement request by contacting
or visiting the Administrative Team. Additional verification may be
required to confirm the student’s identity before the replacement card
is issued.

Students are advised to take proper care of their student cards, as the
card is an important document used for identification and access to
institutional services. Any loss should be reported immediately to avoid
inconvenience.
