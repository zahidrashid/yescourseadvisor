Special Pass Guide for International Students in Malaysia

This guide is based on the provided details and standard procedures for
applying for a Special Pass in Malaysia, which is a temporary pass
(valid for 1 month/30 days) issued when your student pass is about to
expire, has expired, or is under review (e.g., pending renewal, new Visa
Approval Letter/VAL, or while making arrangements to leave the country).
It is a last-resort option at the discretion of the Immigration
Department.

Important Notes:

-   Start the process as early as possible, ideally at least 5-7 working
    days before your student pass expires to avoid overstay fines.
-   If your pass expires before applying, you will incur overstay
    compound fines: RM30 per day for the first 1-30 days after expiry,
    RM1,000 flat for 31-60 days, RM2,000 flat for 61-90 days, and higher
    (determined by Immigration Enforcement) for over 90 days.
-   Based on your details: If within 3 days before expiry, you can apply
    for the Special Pass without immediate fines (valid for 1 month). If
    more than 3 days after expiry, pay the Special Pass fee with
    compound fine of RM30 per day.
-   The application is typically handled through your college’s
    international office or visa processing unit (e.g., meet admin team
    like Ms. Zaty and Ms. Sabreena).
-   A Special Pass can usually be applied for up to twice: first for
    pending decisions, second for leaving the country.
-   If you have any issues, contact Ms. Sabreena or Ms. Zaty for
    assistance.
-   Overstay may require you to attend Immigration appointments, and all
    penalties are your responsibility. Clearing overstay status can take
    4-6 months in some cases.

Required Documents

Gather these based on your situation (valid pass vs. expired/overstay).
Bring originals and photocopies as specified.

-   Original passport (including renewed passport if applicable).
-   Photocopies of passport personal information page and latest student
    pass:
    -   For valid pass: 2 sets.
    -   For overstay 1-90 days: 3 sets.
    -   For overstay >90 days: Photocopies of all passport pages,
        including covers.
-   Explanation letter stating the reason for needing the Special Pass
    (e.g., pending renewal, awaiting VAL, returning home).
-   Proof of flight ticket (printed, in English, showing airline and
    flight number) if returning home or to another country with resident
    pass.
-   Printed online completion/withdrawal form (if you’ve completed,
    withdrawn, or deferred studies; must be approved by your faculty).
-   Format A form (obtain from your college’s visa office/counter).
-   Proof of payment (JomPAY receipt for valid passes or payment
    confirmation for overstay).
-   Any additional college-specific documents (e.g., attendance proof,
    transcript if related to renewal issues).

Fees

-   Base Special Pass fee: RM20 (for student pass holders; pay to EMGS
    for valid passes or Immigration for overstay).
-   Plus overstay compound (if applicable): RM30 per day for 1-30 days,
    RM1,000 for 31-60 days, RM2,000 for 61-90 days, or more for longer
    periods.
-   Example: If overstayed 5 days, total ~ RM20 + (RM30 x 5) = RM170.
-   Payment methods:
    -   For valid passes: Via JomPAY
    -   For overstay: Debit/credit card at EMGS or Immigration (you may
        need to be present; your college will advise).

Step-by-Step Process

-   Assess Your Situation and Prepare Documents Check your student pass
    expiry date. If it’s within 3 days of expiring (or already expired),
    proceed urgently.
    -   Verify eligibility: Ensure you meet basic student requirements
        (e.g., 80% attendance, GPA 2.0+ if tied to renewal).
    -   Gather all required documents listed above.
    -   Write an explanation letter detailing why you need the Special
        Pass (e.g., “My student pass expires in 3 days, and renewal is
        pending.”).
    -   If overstayed, calculate your approximate fine (RM30/day for
        first 30 days) and prepare for payment.
    -   Renew your passport if needed, as the Special Pass will be
        endorsed in it.
-   Contact College Admin Team Go to your college in person to meet the
    admin team (e.g., Ms. Zaty and Ms. Sabreena).
    -   Explain your situation and request assistance with the Special
        Pass application.
    -   They will provide the Format A form and guide you on any
        college-specific steps.
    -   Submit your documents to them at least 5 working days before
        expiry (if possible) to avoid fines.
    -   If you have problems (e.g., missing documents or overstay
        issues), discuss with Ms. Sabreena or Ms. Zaty for solutions or
        alternatives.
-   Make Payment Pay the fees based on your status:
    -   If pass is still valid: Pay RM20 to EMGS via JomPAY and provide
        the receipt.
    -   If overstayed/expired: The college will arrange for you to pay
        at EMGS or Immigration (RM20 + compound fine). You may need to
        attend in person-your admin team will notify you of the date and
        time.
    -   Keep all payment proofs.
-   Submit Application The college admin will submit your application on
    your behalf to EMGS or directly to the Immigration Department (via
    physical submission or online portal).
    -   Hand over your original passport and all documents.
    -   Monitor the status via the EMGS portal if applicable-your
        college can help set this up.
    -   Processing time: Typically 2-3 weeks, but Special Pass issuance
        is quicker (within days if urgent).
-   Collect Passport and Special Pass Once approved, collect your
    passport from the college admin or Immigration (as advised).
    -   The Special Pass will be endorsed in your passport, valid for 1
        month from issuance.
    -   Claim it within 1 week of notification to avoid delays.
    -   Use this time to resolve the underlying issue (e.g., complete
        renewal, obtain VAL, or leave Malaysia).
-   Follow Up and Resolve If the Special Pass is for pending renewal or
    VAL, continue monitoring your main application.
    -   If needed, apply for a second Special Pass (valid another 30
        days) for leaving the country.
    -   If overstayed, attend any required Immigration appointments with
        your college representative.
    -   Ensure you leave Malaysia or regularize your status before the
        Special Pass expires to avoid further penalties.
    -   Contact Ms. Sabreena or Ms. Zaty for any updates or issues.

Note: This is a general guide based on standard Malaysian Immigration
and EMGS procedures for international students. Processes may vary by
institution (e.g., UCSI, UTM). For the most accurate info, refer to your
college’s international office or the official EMGS website: EMGS
Guidelines. If you’re an overstay case, seek immediate advice to
minimize fines. If law enforcement is involved, penalties can include
fines up to RM10,000 or imprisonment. Rendered Special Pass Guide for
