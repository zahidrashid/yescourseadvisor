2. LOAN AMOUNT STRUCTURE
----------------------------------------
Maximum Loan       : RM 8,000 / year
75% Loan Amount    : RM 5,100 / year
50% Loan Amount    : RM 3,400 / year

----------------------------------------
3. SEMESTER INTAKES
----------------------------------------
Semester 1         : January
Semester 2         : May
Semester 3         : September

----------------------------------------

----------------------------------------
Semester 1:
  - Opening Period : December – January
  - Closing Period : December – January

Semester 2:
  - Opening Period : April – May
  - Closing Period : April – May

Semester 3:
  - Opening Period : August – September
  - Closing Period : August – September

----------------------------------------
5. LOAN DISBURSEMENT PROCESS
----------------------------------------
Step 1 : PTPTN sends student data to institution
Step 2 : Institution verifies student status
Step 3 : PTPTN processes payment
Step 4 : Funds credited to student account

----------------------------------------
6. LOAN ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
----------------------------------------
Eligibility is based on:
  • Family income level
  • STR (Sumbangan Tunai Rahmah) status

Categories:
  1. 75% Loan
     - Family income ≤ RM 8,000
     - OR STR recipient

  2. 50% Loan
     - Family income > RM 8,000

----------------------------------------
7. IMPORTANT CONDITIONS
----------------------------------------
  • Loan released after agreement submission
  • Must maintain CGPA ≥ 2.0
  • Must continue in approved programme
  • Must NOT receive other sponsorship

----------------------------------------
8. PAYMENT INFORMATION
----------------------------------------
  • Payment is credited directly to student account
  • PTPTN does NOT deduct tuition fees
  • Students must pay fees to college separately
