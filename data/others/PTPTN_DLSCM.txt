5. LOAN DISBURSEMENT PROCESS
--------------------------------------------------
Step 1 : PTPTN sends student data to institution  
Step 2 : Institution verifies student study status  
Step 3 : PTPTN processes loan payment  
Step 4 : Payment is credited into student’s bank account  

Typical Payment Timeline Example:
- Data Sent to Institution : Early month
- Verification Received    : Mid month
- Payment Processed        : Around 16th / 30th
- Money Credited           : Around 24th / early next month  

--------------------------------------------------
6. LOAN ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
--------------------------------------------------
Eligibility is determined based on:
- Family income level  
- STR (Sumbangan Tunai Rahmah) status  

Loan Categories:

1. 75% Loan
   - Family income ≤ RM 8,000  
   - OR student/guardian is STR recipient  

2. 50% Loan
   - Family income > RM 8,000  
   - Not listed under STR  

Note:
- Maximum loan increased from RM6,800 to RM8,000/year for eligible institutions
- 75% and 50% calculations remain based on standard loan structure  

--------------------------------------------------
7. PROGRAM TYPES
--------------------------------------------------

Full-Time Programme:
- Loan amount based on eligibility criteria  

Part-Time Programme:

  • Open University Malaysia (OUM)

8. IMPORTANT CONDITIONS
--------------------------------------------------
- First payment only released after agreement submission  
- For subsequent payments, student must:
  • Continue in approved programme and institution  
  • Achieve minimum CGPA ≥ 2.0  
  • Not receive any other sponsorship  

--------------------------------------------------
9. PAYMENT INFORMATION
--------------------------------------------------

- Students must handle fee payments with institution
