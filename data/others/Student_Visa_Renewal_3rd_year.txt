Collection Policy for Third-Year Students

All third-year students are required to collect their visa documents in
accordance with the guidelines stated below:

1) Collection within three (3) months

Students are strongly advised to collect their documents within three
(3) months from the date of notification. No collection fee will be
charged if the documents are collected within this period.

2) Collection after three (3) months

If the documents are collected after three (3) months, a storage or
administrative fee of RM100 per month will be imposed. The fee will be
calculated based on the total number of months exceeded.

3) After 100% visa completion

Once the student visa process is 100% completed, students must collect
their documents immediately. Delays after visa completion are not
encouraged and may result in additional administrative issues.

Students are advised to take prompt action and ensure timely collection
to avoid unnecessary charges or complications. For further
clarification, please contact the Administrative Team.
