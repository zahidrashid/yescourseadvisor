How to Get the LRT Letter (Confirmation Letter) for Rapid Pelajar Student Card

The "LRT letter" (also called the Confirmation Letter or NOC letter) is an official letter from your university or college. It confirms that you are a full-time student and states your study duration (must be at least 6 months from the date of application).

This letter is required to apply for or renew the Rapid Pelajar (MyRapid TnG Concession Card), which gives a 50% discount on fares for LRT, MRT, Monorail, BRT, Rapid KL buses, and feeder buses.

Important Update (February 2026)
Starting from 1 January 2026, international students (non-Malaysians) cannot apply for a new card. If you already have a card issued in 2025, you can continue using the 50% discount until the card expires or 31 December 2026 (whichever comes first).

After that, international students will pay full adult fares or use options like the Rapid Bulanan pass (RM150 for unlimited 30-day travel).

Step-by-Step Guide to Get the LRT Letter

1. Go to the Admin / Receptionist Staff
- Visit your university admin office, international student office, or student services counter.
- Request: "Confirmation Letter for Rapid Pelajar / MyRapid Student Concession Card"
- Bring student ID/matric card and passport.

2. Pay the Fee (if required)
- First time: Free
- Re-issue: RM10
- Urgent (same day): RM20

3. Collect the Letter
- Normal: within 3 working days
- Urgent: same day
- Must be original, less than 3 months old, and show study duration more than 6 months

After You Get the Letter

Apply Online:
- Visit www.myrapid.com.my
- Upload photo, ID/passport, student ID, and the letter
- Pay RM15 (RM5 card fee + RM10 stored value)
- Wait 1–3 days for approval

Apply In Person:
- Go to Pasar Seni station (Concession Counter)
- Bring all documents
- Pay RM15 and collect the card
