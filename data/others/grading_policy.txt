GRADING POLICY FOR FINAL EXAMINATION
    1. Purpose This grading policy establishes a fair, transparent, and consistent system for evaluating student performance in final examinations. It converts percentage marks into letter grades and grade points to facilitate academic progression, GPA calculation, and recognition of achievement.
    2. Scope This policy applies to all final examinations across all subjects and programs unless a specific course has an approved variation (subject to departmental approval).
    3. Grade Point Average (GPA) and Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) Calculation
Each subject is assigned credit hours based on its workload. GPA (for a single semester) and CGPA (across all semesters) are calculated as: GPA/CGPA = (Σ (Grade Points × Credit Hours)) ÷ (Total Credit Hours Attempted) Results are rounded to two decimal places. Failed subjects (F) contribute 0.00 grade points but are included in total credit hours attempted.
    1. Additional Policies
    • Rounding of Marks: Individual subject percentage marks are rounded to the nearest whole number before applying the scale (e.g., 79.5% → 80%, earning A-).
    • Grace Marks: Up to 2 grace marks may be awarded in exceptional cases (e.g., to reach the passing threshold or next grade boundary), subject to departmental committee approval.
    • Re-evaluation/Rechecking: Students may apply for re-evaluation within 10 days of result declaration, subject to payment of prescribed fees.
    • Academic Probation: Students with semester GPA below 1.50 may be placed on academic probation.
    • Incomplete Grades: Temporary "I" (Incomplete) grades may be assigned for valid reasons; must be resolved within the next semester.
