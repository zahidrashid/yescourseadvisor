5. LOAN DISBURSEMENT PROCESS
--------------------------------------------------
Step 1 : PTPTN sends student data to institution  
Step 2 : Institution verifies student status  
Step 3 : PTPTN processes loan payment  
Step 4 : Funds are credited into student’s bank account  

Typical Timeline:
- Data sent to institution    : Early month  
- Status confirmed by IPT     : Mid month  
- Payment processed           : Around 16th / 30th  
- Credit to account           : Around 24th / early next month  
【1-3d5027】

--------------------------------------------------
6. LOAN ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
--------------------------------------------------
Eligibility is based on:
- Family income level  
- STR (Sumbangan Tunai Rahmah) status  

Loan Categories:

1. 75% Loan
   - Family income ≤ RM 8,000  
   - OR student/guardian is STR recipient  

2. 50% Loan
   - Family income > RM 8,000  
   - Not listed under STR  

Note:
- Maximum loan increased from RM6,800 to RM8,000/year  
- 75% and 50% remain proportional to loan structure【1-3d5027】
