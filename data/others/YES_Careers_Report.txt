YES INTERNATIONAL COLLEGE CAREERS REPORT

1. INTRODUCTION
YES International College is dedicated to shaping the future of education through high-quality, industry-relevant programmes. The institution fosters a dynamic, innovative, and supportive working environment where professionals can grow, contribute, and inspire students.

2. ORGANISATIONAL VALUES
- Commitment to academic excellence
- Industry-relevant education
- Supportive and collaborative workplace
- Professional growth and innovation
- Focus on student success and development

3. CAREER OPPORTUNITIES

3.1 Academic Positions
YES International College seeks passionate educators in the following disciplines:
- Graphic Design
- Business Management
- Logistics
- Information Technology
- English
- Make-up Studies
- Photography

Key Requirements:
- Strong subject expertise
- Passion for teaching
- Effective communication skills

3.2 Administrative Positions
Administrative roles are available across multiple departments:
- Student Services
- Admissions
- Finance
- Human Resources
- Academic Administration

Key Benefits:
- Competitive salary package
- Education subsidies (staff and family)
- Interest-free loans
- Medical coverage
- Professional development opportunities
- Collaborative and balanced work environment

3.3 Sales and Marketing Positions
The Sales & Marketing team plays a crucial role in student recruitment and brand promotion.

Key Responsibilities:
- Promote programmes locally and internationally
- Engage with education agents and partners
- Build recruitment networks
- Represent the YES brand professionally

Key Benefits:
- Competitive basic salary
- Attractive commission structure
- High earning potential

Key Requirements:
- Strong interpersonal and negotiation skills
- Results-driven attitude

3.4 Internship Programme
YES International College offers a structured interns-to-hire programme.

Departments Available:
- Academics
- Administration
- Marketing
- IT Support
- Student Services

Programme Benefits:
- Paid internships
- Real-world industry experience
- Professional mentorship
- Pathway to full-time employment

Target Applicants:
- Final-year students
- Fresh graduates

YES International College provides diverse career opportunities within a professional and supportive environment. With strong benefits, career progression pathways, and a commitment to excellence, it is an ideal workplace for individuals passionate about education and professional growth.
