Step-by-Step Guide for Enrolling as an International Student in Malaysia

This guide provides a comprehensive overview for international students
enrolling in Malaysian universities or colleges (primarily Private
Higher Educational Institutions or IPTS; note that Public Institutions
or IPTA may have different processes). The process involves document
preparation, eligibility checks, visa application through the Education
Malaysia Global Services (EMGS) portal, arrival procedures, and
registration. Start early, as the entire process can take 2-6 months.

Key Notes:

-   All fees are approximate and in Malaysian Ringgit (RM). Prices may
    vary by institution-confirm with your chosen university/college.
-   Miscellaneous/initial fees (visa, registration, administrative, and
    1st year/semester tuition) must be paid before flying to Malaysia.
    The cheapest total cost is around RM15,000 (excluding flight ticket,
    Single Entry Visa, and accommodation).
-   Universities handle much of the visa process via EMGS for IPTS
    students.
-   If you encounter issues, contact your university’s international
    office or counsellor.
-   Refer to official sources like EMGS website for the latest updates.

Required Documents

Prepare these in advance. Scans or originals may be needed at different
stages-ensure they are clear and valid.

-   Passport: Full pages (scanned or photocopied), valid for at least 18
    months from enrollment date.
-   Passport-Size Photo: With white background (typically 3.5cm x 4.5cm
    or 3.5cm x 5cm; digital version for applications).
-   Academic Certificates and Transcripts:
    -   For Diploma programs.
    -   For Bachelor’s programs.
-   Student’s Personal Details: Home country address, father’s or
    mother’s information (name, contact, occupation-often for forms or
    financial proof).
-   English Proficiency Certificate: PTE, IELTS, Linguaskill, or
    equivalent (required for eligibility check).
-   Additional as Needed: Experience letter (if study gap >3 years),
    financial statements (bank statements or sponsor affidavits showing
    sufficient funds, e.g., RM10,000-20,000+ depending on program).

Step-by-Step Process

-   Research and Apply to a University/College Choose an IPTS
    institution (e.g., via EMGS-approved list). Submit an online
    application through the university’s portal.
    -   Provide basic details and upload initial documents (passport,
        photo, academic certificates/transcripts, personal info).
    -   Pay any application fee (varies, ~RM500-1,000).
    -   Await preliminary review.
-   Eligibility Checking The university counsellor reviews your
    application.
    -   They check CGPA (minimum varies by program, e.g., 2.0+),
        academic qualifications, and English proficiency (e.g., IELTS
        5.5+, PTE 42+, Linguaskill equivalent).
    -   If eligible, an Offer Letter is issued (conditional or
        unconditional). This confirms your acceptance and outlines fees.
    -   If ineligible (e.g., low CGPA or no English certificate), you
        may need to retake exams or provide more docs-reapply if needed.
-   Pay Initial Fees and Start Visa Application Accept the Offer Letter
    and pay miscellaneous/initial fees before visa processing. This
    includes:
    -   Visa fee: RM2,300 (1 year) to RM3,500 (3 years), varies by
        institution.
    -   Registration fee, administrative fee, and 1st year/semester
        tuition (total ~RM15,000 minimum for cheapest options).
    -   The university applies for your Student Pass/Visa on your behalf
        via the EMGS portal (for IPTS only; IPTA uses different
        systems).
    -   Monitor progress in the EMGS portal (0-70% stage): Upload any
        requested docs (e.g., experience letter for >3-year study gap,
        family/student financial statements).
    -   Processing time: 4-8 weeks; officers may request clarifications.
-   Receive 70% Approval and Apply for Single Entry Visa (SEV) Once EMGS
    reaches 70% (visa approved in principle), download or request the
    Electronic Visa Approval Letter (eVAL) from the university/EMGS.
    -   Apply for SEV at a Malaysian embassy/consulate in your home
        country (fee: ~RM120).
    -   Provide eVAL, passport, photo, and other docs.
    -   Some nationalities (e.g., Bangladesh) require an embassy
        interview-book in advance.
    -   Processing: 1-2 weeks. Once approved, you get an e-Single Entry
        Visa.
-   Prepare for Travel After SEV approval:
    -   Book a flight ticket to Malaysia.
    -   Complete the Malaysian Digital Arrival Card (MDAC) online (<3
        days before arrival) via imd.gov.my.
    -   Submit to university’s international department: e-Single Entry
        Visa, eVAL, flight ticket, and MDAC for airport clearance
        processing.
    -   Print all documents: Passport, eVAL, SEV, flight ticket, MDAC,
        Offer Letter, and any financial/academic proofs.
    -   Ensure initial fees are fully paid.
-   Arrival in Malaysia (Touched Down) Upon landing (e.g., at KLIA):
    -   Proceed to the immigration counter for self-clearance.
    -   Present all printed documents to the officer.
    -   Get an entry stamp in your passport (temporary social visit
        pass, valid ~30-90 days).
    -   If issues arise, contact your university representative (they
        may meet you or assist remotely).
    -   Head to your university/accommodation.
-   Medical Check-Up Within 7-14 days of arrival:
    -   Schedule a medical examination at an EMGS-approved panel clinic
        (list in EMGS portal; university can guide).
    -   Tests include: Blood, urine, HIV, disease screening, Hepatitis
        B.
    -   Cost: Free (covered in visa fee).
    -   Receive a medical slip/report at the end.
    -   If you fail (e.g., health issues), you must return home
        immediately but can reapply after 3 months.
-   University Registration and Visa Endorsement
    -   Submit your passport, medical slip, and any remaining docs to
        the university’s international office.
    -   Register officially (pay any balance fees if needed).
    -   University handles Student Pass endorsement: Submit to
        EMGS/Immigration.
    -   Monitor EMGS portal: Progress from 70% to 100% (visa fully
        approved). Results may appear in university’s portal (e.g.,
        STARS).
    -   Processing: 3-4 weeks. Collect your passport with the endorsed
        Student Pass sticker.
    -   You can now legally stay and study.
-   Ongoing Requirements
    -   Renew your Student Pass yearly (start 3-4 months before expiry;
        similar process, fees ~RM1,000-2,000).
    -   Maintain eligibility: 80% attendance, GPA 2.0+, etc.
    -   For accommodation, flights, or other costs: Budget extra (e.g.,
        accommodation ~RM500-1,500/month, flights vary by origin).
    -   If changing programs or institutions, apply for pass
        shortening/cancellation first.

Final Tips: Work closely with your university’s international team-they
handle most submissions. Track everything via EMGS app/portal. Costs
exclude living expenses (~RM1,500-3,000/month). If from restricted
nationalities, expect extra scrutiny. Consult EMGS or Malaysian
Immigration for personalized advice.
