CORPORATE TRAINING PROGRAM
"Know Yourself, Know Others"
==================================================

1. PROGRAM OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------
Program Type        : Corporate Training
Focus               : Self-awareness, communication, teamwork, emotional intelligence

Description:
This training program is designed to enhance self-awareness and interpersonal skills, 
promoting collaboration and productivity in the workplace. It uses engaging activities 
and discussions to develop empathy, communication, and teamwork.

Objectives:
- Improve workplace communication and collaboration
- Strengthen emotional intelligence and empathy
- Enhance teamwork and trust
- Increase productivity and job satisfaction

--------------------------------------------------

- Empathy & Trust Building
  • Strengthen relationships through effective communication

- Practical Application
  • Apply skills to real-world workplace situations

  • Based on Kolb’s Experiential Learning Cycle

3. TRAINING METHODOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------
Core Approach: Experiential Learning

Kolb’s Experiential Learning Cycle:
1. Concrete Experience (Feeling)
   - Participate in real activities

2. Reflective Observation (Watching)
   - Observe and discuss experiences

3. Abstract Conceptualisation (Thinking)
   - Analyse and understand concepts

4. Active Experimentation (Doing)
   - Apply knowledge in real-world scenarios
【1-de70ba】

7. PROGRAM SCHEDULE (SAMPLE – FULL DAY)
--------------------------------------------------

09:00 – Opening & Icebreaker
- Program introduction and rules
- Team formation activity (name, slogan, leader)

09:30 – Blind Spots Activity
- Method:
  • Blindfold communication task
- Objectives:
  • Communication, trust, adaptability, problem-solving

10:30 – Break

10:45 – Puzzle Challenge
- Method:
  • Timed team puzzle activity
- Objectives:
  • Teamwork, strategy, cooperation

12:15 – Lunch Break

13:15 – Magic Hula Hoop
- Method:
  • Group coordination using fingers
- Objectives:
  • Focus, coordination, teamwork

14:00 – The Tiny Dance
- Method:
  • Group performance activity
- Objectives:
  • Creativity, confidence, collaboration

15:15 – Break

15:30 – DISC Personality Workshop
- Method:
  • Personality profiling and group discussion
- Objectives:
  • Understanding differences, empathy, teamwork

16:30 – Magic Hat Feedback Circle
- Method:
  • Feedback sharing session
- Objectives:
  • Self-awareness, appreciation, communication

17:15 – Dominoes Team Challenge
- Method:
  • Group design/word creation activity
- Objectives:
  • Collaboration and recognizing contributions

17:45 – Conclusion & Group Photo

- Reflection and closing session
