Nearby Accommodation Options (Rumah Sewa / Room Rentals)
YES International College is located in a prime area of Kuala Lumpur, within walking distance of the Petronas Twin Towers, Suria KLCC, and nearby LRT/MRT stations. Many students choose to rent rooms, studios, or shared apartments nearby for convenience.

Popular Areas: Jalan Yap Kwan Seng, KLCC, Ampang, and Bukit Bintang (approximately 5–20 minutes by walking or commuting).
Typical Prices (2026 estimates):
Single/shared room (basic, shared bathroom): RM800–1,500/month
Studio or medium-sized room with air conditioning and furnishings: RM1,200–2,500+/month
Popular Buildings/Condominiums: Star Residences, Royce Residence, The Mews, Scarletz Suites, Taragon Puteri YKS, and Mirage Residence (many listings are available on the platforms below).
Recommended Platforms to Search:
iBilik.com – Great for student rooms near KLCC and Jalan Yap Kwan Seng.
Mudah.my – Search for "room for rent KLCC" or "Jalan Yap Kwan Seng" to find direct owner listings.
PropertyGuru.com.my – Ideal for condos and serviced apartments, with many available options in the area.
uhomes.com/my/kuala-lumpur – Student-focused accommodation, including options near KLCC.
Tips: Check whether utilities, WiFi, and security are included in the rental. Gated condominiums are generally a good choice for safety. If your budget is below RM1,000 per month, consider shared rooms in nearby areas such as Ampang or Bukit Bintang.
