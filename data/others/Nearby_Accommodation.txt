Nearby Accommodation Options (Rumah Sewa / Room Rentals)
YES International College is in a prime KLCC location (walking distance to Petronas Twin Towers, Suria KLCC, and LRT/MRT stations). Many students choose rumah sewa (rooms, studios, or shared apartments) nearby for convenience.
    • Prime Areas: Jalan Yap Kwan Seng, KLCC fringe, Ampang, Bukit Bintang (5–20 min walk/commute).
    • Typical Prices (2026 estimates):
        ◦ Single/shared room (basic, shared bathroom): RM 800–1,500/month
        ◦ Studio/medium room with AC & furnishing: RM 1,200–2,500+/month
    • Popular buildings/condos: Star Residences, Royce Residence, The Mews, Scarletz Suites, Taragon Puteri YKS, Mirage Residence (many listings on platforms below).
    • Recommended Platforms to Search:
        ◦ iBilik.com → Great for student rooms near KLCC/Jalan Yap Kwan Seng (filter "KLCC" or "Jalan Yap Kwan Seng").
        ◦ Mudah.my → Search "room for rent KLCC" or "Jalan Yap Kwan Seng" (lots of direct owner listings).
        ◦ PropertyGuru.com.my → For condos/serviced apartments (hundreds of options in the area).
        ◦ uhomes.com/my/kuala-lumpur → Student-focused housing, some near KLCC.
    • Tips: Look for inclusions (utilities, WiFi, security), gated condos for safety, and proximity to Ampang Park or KLCC LRT for easy college access. Budget under RM1,000? Check shared rooms in nearby Ampang or Bukit Bintang.
