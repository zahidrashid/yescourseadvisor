How to Calculate CGPA Based on Subject Grades (Using the Provided Grading Scale)
CGPA stands for Cumulative Grade Point Average. It is a measure of your overall academic performance across multiple subjects (or semesters). In the grading system you shared, each subject's final exam mark is converted to a letter grade and then to grade points (on a 4.0 scale). The CGPA is calculated by weighting these grade points by the credit hours of each subject.
Step-by-Step Guide to Calculate CGPA
    1. Determine the Grade Points for Each Subject
Use the grading scale to convert your percentage mark to grade points.
Marks Range	Letter Grade	Grade Points
80–100	A	4.00
75–79	A-	3.67
70–74	B+	3.33
65–69	B	3.00
60–64	B-	2.67
55–59	C+	2.33
50–54	C	2.00
47–49	C-	1.67
44–46	D+	1.33
40–43	D	1.00
0–39	F	0.00
       
    2. Know the Credit Hours for Each Subject
Every subject has a certain number of credits (also called credit hours or units). This reflects the workload or importance of the subject.
        ◦ Common values: 3 credits (major subjects), 2 credits (labs or minor subjects), 1 credit (electives), etc.
        ◦ If all subjects have the same credits (e.g., all 3 credits), the calculation becomes simpler.
    3. Calculate Grade Points Earned for Each Subject
Formula:
Grade Points Earned = Grade Points × Credit Hours
    4. Sum Up Total Grade Points Earned and Total Credit Hours
        ◦ Total Grade Points Earned = Sum of (Grade Points × Credits) for all subjects
        ◦ Total Credit Hours = Sum of credits for all subjects attempted
    5. Calculate CGPA
Final Formula:
CGPA = Total Grade Points Earned ÷ Total Credit Hours
(Usually rounded to 2 decimal places)
Detailed Example
Suppose you have taken 5 subjects in a semester:
Subject	Marks (%)	Letter Grade	Grade Points	Credit Hours	Grade Points Earned (Grade Points × Credits)
Mathematics	85	A	4.00	4	4.00 × 4 = 16.00
Physics	72	B+	3.33	3	3.33 × 3 = 9.99
Chemistry	68	B	3.00	3	3.00 × 3 = 9.00
English	58	C+	2.33	2	2.33 × 2 = 4.66
Computer Science	45	D+	1.33	3	1.33 × 3 = 3.99

Step 1: Add up Total Grade Points Earned
16.00 + 9.99 + 9.00 + 4.66 + 3.99 = 43.64
Step 2: Add up Total Credit Hours
4 + 3 + 3 + 2 + 3 = 15
Step 3: CGPA = 43.64 ÷ 15 = 2.91 (approximately)
Your semester GPA (or CGPA if this is your only semester) would be 2.91 out of 4.00.
For Multiple Semesters (True Cumulative GPA)
If you have results from more than one semester:
    • Calculate the Total Grade Points Earned across all semesters.
    • Calculate the Total Credit Hours across all semesters.
    • Then apply the same formula: CGPA = Total Grade Points ÷ Total Credit Hours.
Special Cases
    • Failed Subject (F): Grade points = 0.00. You earn no points, but the credits are still counted in the total (this lowers your CGPA).
    • Retaken Subject: Some universities replace the old grade, others average both — check your institution's policy.
    • All Subjects Same Credits: If every subject is worth the same credits, you can simply average the grade points directly.
Quick Tips
    • Aim for higher marks in subjects with more credit hours — they impact your CGPA more.
    • A CGPA of 3.50+ is often considered good/first division in many systems.
    • Use a spreadsheet (Excel/Google Sheets) for easy calculation with many subjects.
