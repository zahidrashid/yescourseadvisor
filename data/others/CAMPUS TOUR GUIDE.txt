==================================================
YES INTERNATIONAL COLLEGE
CAMPUS TOUR GUIDE
==================================================

1. OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------
This guide provides a structured walkthrough of YES International College 
campus. It can be used for self-guided tours or to assist visitors in 
understanding the campus layout, facilities, and key services.

Purpose:
- Introduce campus facilities
- Provide a clear navigation route
- Highlight academic and student support areas
【1-8639d2】

--------------------------------------------------
2. STARTING POINT: UG FLOOR (UPPER GROUND)
--------------------------------------------------
Key Areas:
- Reception Area
  • Main point for check-in and inquiries
  • Obtain campus maps and brochures

- Library
  • Quiet study environment
  • Equipped with books, computers, and study tables

Tour Tips:
- Ask reception for access permissions if needed
- Collect available brochures or event schedules
【1-8639d2】

--------------------------------------------------
3. LOWER GROUND FLOOR (LG FLOOR)
--------------------------------------------------
Access:
- Use stairs from UG Floor

Key Facilities:
- Glass Classrooms
  • C-LG-1 and C-LG-2
  • Modern classrooms with transparent walls
  • Used for lectures and workshops

Tour Tips:
- Walk along corridors to observe teaching setups
- Check if classrooms are available for viewing
【1-8639d2】

--------------------------------------------------
4. 6TH FLOOR (MAIN ACADEMIC AREA)
--------------------------------------------------
Key Areas:
- Academic Office (Registrar & Administration)
  • Handles enrolment, documents, and academic inquiries

- Student Lounge
  • Relaxation and study area
  • Equipped with seating and vending machines

- Mini Mock Room (Hotel & Tourism)
  • Simulated hotel room for hands-on training

- Classrooms
- Exam Room

Tour Tips:
- Visit Academic Office for course information
- Explore the Mini Mock Room for practical learning insights
- Observe student interaction in the lounge
【1-8639d2】

5. 8TH FLOOR (QUIET STUDY AREA)
--------------------------------------------------
Key Facilities:

  • C-803

Tour Tips:
- Ideal floor to experience a quieter academic setting

9. CAMPUS EXPERIENCE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------
The YES International College campus tour offers a complete view of:

- Student services and administrative support (UG Floor)
- Modern classrooms and lecture spaces (LG, 8th Floor)
- Academic and practical learning areas (6th & 18th Floor)
- Specialized training facilities (Block B)

Outcome:
Visitors gain a clear understanding of the college’s:
- Learning environment
- Academic resources
- Practical training approach
- Student-centered atmosphere
