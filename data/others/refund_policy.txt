Refund Policy for International Students

Refund Policy Details

International Student Fee

The International Student Fee is non-refundable under any circumstances.

Visa Rejection by the Malaysian Immigration Department (International Students Only)

In the event that an international student’s visa application is
rejected by the Malaysian Immigration Department, the fees paid are
refundable. However, the refund is subject to the refundable amount
determined by EMGS (Education Malaysia Global Services). Certain
deductions may apply as per EMGS guidelines.

Withdrawal Following Commencement of the Course (All Students)

If a student withdraws from the course after the official commencement
date, no refund will be provided for any fees already paid. The first
day of orientation is officially deemed the commencement day of the
course. Therefore, any withdrawal requested on or after this date will
not qualify for a refund.

Expulsion or Suspension Due to Misconduct (All Students)

In the event that a student is expelled and/or suspended from the course
due to academic misconduct, breach of the student’s code of conduct, or
violation of the dress code, no refund will be granted. Furthermore, the
tuition fee for the current semester will remain fully due and payable
to the college, regardless of the expulsion or suspension.
