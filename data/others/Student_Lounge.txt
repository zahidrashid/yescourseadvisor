Student Lounge / Chill & Study Area Students are welcome to relax,
study, and spend their free time at the Student Lounge / Chill Area,
which is designed to provide a comfortable and friendly environment for
both leisure and academic activities.

The Student Lounge, also known as the study and chill area, is located
on Level 6. This space is open to all students and may be used for
self‑study, group discussions, resting, or casual social interaction.
Students are encouraged to make good use of this area for productive and
positive activities during breaks or after classes.

Please ensure that the lounge area is kept clean, quiet, and respectful
at all times so that it remains a pleasant environment for everyone.
