 NOC Letter - confirmation letter for apply visa travel

For international students (and sometimes local students) in Malaysian universities/colleges, a Confirmation Letter (commonly referred to as NOC Letter in student contexts) is an official document from your institution. It verifies your enrollment status, study duration, and other details. This letter is typically needed for various purposes, such as:

Visa/Student Pass-related processes (e.g., for specific nationalities like Sudan, where it's a true NOC from the embassy, handled by the university).
Other administrative needs (e.g., transport concessions, scholarships, or external verifications—though some restrictions apply in 2026).

The fees for issuing this Confirmation Letter vary by university, but based on common practices at many institutions (especially private colleges and some public ones), here's the general fee structure you mentioned:
Fee Structure for Confirmation Letter / NOC Letter

First time application: Free (no charge for the initial request).
Second time or re-issue (e.g., lost, additional copy, or re-application): RM10 (normal processing; collect within 3 working days).
Urgent / Same-day issuance: RM20 (extra fee for priority processing and immediate collection).

Student just needs to pay RM10 for a standard second-time or re-issued Confirmation/NOC Letter (if the first one was free). For urgent needs, pay RM20 to get it the same day.

General Step-by-Step to Get the Confirmation/NOC Letter

1. Visit the Admin/Reception Counter
Go in person to your university/college's admin office, international student office, student services counter, or receptionist staff (e.g., the team that handles student letters and verifications).

2. Request the Letter
Ask for a Confirmation Letter (or specifically NOC Letter if it's for visa/embassy purposes). 

Provide your:
- Student ID / Matric card.
- Passport (for international students).
- Reason for the letter (e.g., visa renewal, external requirement).
- They may have an online form/portal in some unis (e.g., MAYA at UM), but often it's in-person.

3. Pay the Applicable Fee

- Fee: RM10.
Urgent (same-day): RM20.
Pay at the counter (cash or as per university policy). The staff will process it accordingly.

3. Collect the Letter
Normal: Within 3 working days.
Urgent: Same day.
The letter will be printed on official letterhead, stamped, and signed.
