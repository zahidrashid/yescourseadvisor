Resource Fee Payment Policy All students are required to pay the
Resource Fee as part of their academic and administrative requirements.
The payment structure is as follows:

Annual Resource Fee Students are required to pay a Resource Fee of
RM900, which covers one (1) academic year. This fee supports access to
academic resources, facilities, and student services provided by the
institution.

Semester-Based Resource Fee Alternatively, students may opt to pay the
Resource Fee on a semester basis. In this case, a payment of RM300 per
semester is required. Students who choose this option must ensure that
the payment is made at the beginning of each semester.

Students are reminded that the Resource Fee must be fully settled to
maintain active student status and to continue accessing institutional
services and facilities. Failure to make timely payment may result in
administrative delays or restrictions. For any questions or financial
concerns regarding the Resource Fee, students are advised to contact or
meet with the Administrative Team for further assistance.
