Student visa renewal is required on an annual basis. Students must
ensure that their passport is submitted at least three (3) months before
the student visa expiration date. The passport must be submitted and
sent to the college for visa renewal processing. Failure to comply with
this timeline may result in delays or visa-related issues.To avoid
incurring Special Pass fees and fines imposed by Immigration
authorities, students must comply with all visa renewal procedures and
timelines. Timely submission of required documents is essential to
prevent penalties or legal complications. Meet the Administrative Team
for International Student Services:

Ms. Sabrina – Administrative Executive Ms. Zati Ms. Ziela

The above team members are responsible for handling matters related to
international student services. Students may approach them for
assistance regarding administrative and visa-related inquiries. Please
ensure that all outstanding payments are fully settled before collecting
your passport, insurance card, student ID card, and i‑Kad. Students who
have any financial concerns or difficulties are advised to meet with the
Administrative Team mentioned above for further assistance and guidance.
