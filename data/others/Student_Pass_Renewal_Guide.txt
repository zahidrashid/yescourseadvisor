### Student Pass Renewal Guide

This guide is based on the provided requirements for renewing your
student pass (likely a student visa in Malaysia). Ensure you start the
process **4 months before your current pass expires** and before
arriving at the college. Key prerequisites include:

- **Attendance**: Must be 80% or above for all classes.

- **Academic Performance**: Transcript with a GPA of 2.0 or above
(ensure all subjects are passed).

- **English Competency**: A valid certificate from an approved external
exam (taken outside the college).

- **Passport**: Use your renewed passport for the application.

If you encounter any issues, contact the admin team: Ms. Zaty or
Ms. Sabreena.

## Required Documents

- Renewed passport.

- Proof of attendance (80% or above).

- Academic transcript (GPA 2.0+).

- English competency certificate from one of the following exams (taken
externally):

- TOEFL

- MUET

- IELTS

- PTE

- Linguaskill

- Any other college-specific forms or supporting documents.

## Step-by-Step Process

1. **Prepare and Verify Requirements**

Ensure you meet all prerequisites: attendance ≥80%, GPA ≥2.0, and obtain
your English competency certificate.

- **Suggestion for English Exam**: If choosing TOEFL, consider taking it
in Petaling Jaya, Malaysia. You can find test centers and registration
via the official ETS TOEFL website: [ETS TOEFL
Registration](https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/register.html).
Search for centers in Petaling Jaya during registration.

- Links to other exams:

- [MUET (Malaysian University English
Test)](https://www.mpm.edu.my/en/student/muet)

- [IELTS](https://www.ielts.org/)

- [PTE (Pearson Test of English)](https://www.pearsonpte.com/)

-
[Linguaskill](https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/exams-and-tests/linguaskill/)

Renew your passport if it’s expiring soon, as you’ll need the renewed
one for the application.

2. **Visit the College and Meet Admin Team**

Go to the college in person to initiate the renewal process. Meet with
the admin team, specifically Ms. Zaty and Ms. Sabreena.

- Bring all required documents listed above.

- Discuss your application and submit the necessary forms. They will
guide you on any additional steps specific to your situation.

3. **Submit English Competency Certificate**

Provide your certificate from the chosen exam (e.g., TOEFL, MUET, etc.).
Ensure it’s valid and from an external provider.

4. **Address Any Issues**

If there are problems with your documents, requirements, or application
(e.g., low attendance or GPA issues), proceed directly to Ms. Sabreena
or Ms. Zaty for assistance. They can provide advice or alternatives.

5. **Complete Submission and Wait for Approval**

Once all documents are submitted and requirements met, the college will
process your renewal. Follow up with the admin team if needed.

- The process should be started 4 months prior to expiry to avoid
delays.

**Note**: This is a general outline based on your details. Contact your
college’s international office for the most accurate and updated
information, as requirements can vary. If your pass is for Malaysia,
refer to EMGS (Education Malaysia Global Services) for official student
pass renewal guidelines: [EMGS Student Pass
Renewal](https://visa.educationmalaysia.gov.my/guidelines/student-pass-renewal.html).
