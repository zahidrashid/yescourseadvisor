DIPLOMA IN PSYCHOLOGY

SECTION A: PROGRAMME INFORMATION
--------------------------------
Programme Course: Diploma in Psychology
Programme Code: MQA/PA18603
Programme Summary:
A 2.5-year diploma programme that provides foundational knowledge and practical skills in psychology, preparing students for further studies or entry-level roles in psychology-related fields.

Awarding Body: YES International College
Duration: 2 years & 3 months (7 semesters + internship)
Mode of Study: Full-time
Intake Months: January, May, September

Entry Requirements:
- SPM: Minimum 3 credits + pass in Mathematics and Science subject
- STPM: Minimum Grade C + pass in Mathematics and Science (SPM level)
- STAM: Minimum Maqbul + pass in Mathematics and Science (SPM level)
- Certificate (Level 3 MQF, CGPA ≥ 2.00) + pass in Mathematics and Science
- SKM Level 3 (with bridging requirements if needed)
- Other recognised equivalent qualifications
