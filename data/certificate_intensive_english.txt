Programme Summary:
The Certificate in Intensive English is designed to rapidly improve students’ English proficiency through focused, high-intensity training. The programme emphasizes practical communication skills, including speaking, listening, reading, and writing, alongside grammar and vocabulary development. It is suitable for learners who need fast-track improvement for academic, professional, or personal purposes.
