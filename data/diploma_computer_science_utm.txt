DIPLOMA IN COMPUTER SCIENCE (UTM COLLABORATION)

SECTION A: PROGRAMME INFORMATION
--------------------------------
Programme Course: Diploma in Computer Science in Collaboration with UTM
Programme Code: N/0613/4/0023 (12/28) MQA/PA16689
Programme Summary:
A 3-year diploma programme based on Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM) curriculum. It provides a comprehensive foundation in computing, including programming, systems development, databases, networking, and security. The programme develops both practical and analytical skills for real-world IT applications.

Awarding Body: Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM)
Duration: Minimum 2.5 years (5 semesters), Maximum 4 years (8 semesters)
Mode of Study: Full-time
Intake Months: January, May, September

Entry Requirements:
- SPM or equivalent (Pass in Bahasa Melayu & History)
- Minimum 3 credits including Mathematics
- Credit or pass in English
- SKM/KKM Level 3 (CGPA ≥ 2.00) accepted
- Students without Maths credit must complete equivalent before progression
