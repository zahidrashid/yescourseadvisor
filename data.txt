YES International College Registry Department Overview
The Registry Department at YES International College is responsible for managing student administrative services, from admission to graduation. It is headed by Ms Norhanim Mohsin and provides essential support to both local and international students. The department ensures smooth academic progression by handling records, loans, visas, and other student-related processes.
