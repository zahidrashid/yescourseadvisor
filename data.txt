========================================
PROGRAMME INFORMATION
========================================

[A01] Programme / Course
Association of Chartered Certified Accountants (ACCA)

[A02] Programme Code
MQA/FA8401

[A03] Programme Summary
The Association of Chartered Certified Accountants (ACCA) is a professional qualification in accounting and finance that prepares students for careers in auditing, taxation, financial management, accounting, and business strategy.

[A04] Awarding Body
Association of Chartered Certified Accountants

[A05] Duration
3 Years / 6 Semesters

[A06] Mode of Study
Online, Full-time, Part-time

[A07] Intake Months
1. January
2. July

[A08] Entry Requirements
• SPM with 3 credits including English and Mathematics
• Or equivalent qualifications
