DIPLOMA IN INFORMATION SYSTEMS

SECTION A: PROGRAMME INFORMATION
--------------------------------
Programme Course: Diploma in Information Systems
Programme Code: R/482/4/0185 (MQA/FA 10562-01)
Programme Summary:
A 2.5-year diploma programme that equips students with knowledge and practical skills to design and manage information systems. Covers systems analysis, databases, programming, and web technologies with a focus on solving business problems.

Awarding Body: YES International College
Duration: 2.5 years (7 semesters + internship)
Mode of Study: Full-time
Intake Months: January, May, September

Entry Requirements:
- Local: SPM/STPM/STAM/SKM Level 3 (min 3 credits including Mathematics)
- International: MUET Band 3 / CEFR B1 or equivalent
