HIGHER NATIONAL DIPLOMA (HND) IN GRAPHIC DESIGN

SECTION A: PROGRAMME INFORMATION
--------------------------------
Programme Course: Higher National Diploma in Art & Design (Graphic Design)
Programme Code: 603/0908/8

Programme Summary:
A 2-year UK-based qualification that develops professional graphic design skills through practice-based learning. The programme covers visual communication, typography, branding, digital media, and contextual studies, preparing graduates for industry roles or degree progression.

Awarding Body: Pearson BTEC (UK)
Duration: 2 Years
Mode of Study: Full-time
Intake Months: January, May, September

Entry Requirements:
- SPM / O-Level / UEC
- OR equivalent qualifications

SECTION B: PROGRAMME STRUCTURE
--------------------------------
Total Credit Hours: 240 credits

Main Subjects Overview:
- Graphic Design Practice
- Typography
- Branding & Identity
- Packaging Design
- Visual Merchandising
- Digital Media & Animation
- Contextual & Professional Studies

Level 4 Units:
- Unit 1 – Professional Development
- Unit 2 – Contextual Studies
- Unit 3 – Individual Project
- Unit 4 – Techniques & Processes
- Unit 4 – Typography
- Unit 13 – Graphic Design Practices
- Unit 19 – Packaging Design
- Unit 24 – Visual Merchandising

Level 5 Units:
- Unit 32 – Professional Practice
- Unit 33 – Applied Practice Collaborative Project
- Unit 39 – Advanced Graphic Design Studies
- Unit 47 – Branding & Identity
- Unit 52 – Moving Image
- Unit 60 – Digital Animation

SECTION C: STUDY MODE & ASSESSMENT
--------------------------------
Overall Study Mode:
- Lectures, tutorials, workshops, and project-based learning

Assessment:
- 100% coursework-based (projects, portfolios, reports, presentations)
- Turnitin submission required (low similarity expected)

Grading System:
- Pass (P)
- Merit (M)
- Distinction (D)

Degree Progression:
