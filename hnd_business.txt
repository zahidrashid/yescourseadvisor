HIGHER NATIONAL DIPLOMA (HND) IN BUSINESS

SECTION A: PROGRAMME INFORMATION
--------------------------------
Programme Course: Higher National Diploma in Business
Programme Code:
- Pearson BTEC Level 4: 603/6837/8
- Pearson BTEC Level 5: 603/6838/X

Programme Summary:
A 2-year UK-based qualification focusing on business studies and applied learning. The programme emphasizes real-world case studies, projects, and assignments to develop critical thinking, problem-solving, communication, and decision-making skills required in business environments.

Awarding Body: Pearson BTEC (UK)
Duration: 2 Years
Mode of Study: Full-time
Intake Months: January, May, September

Entry Requirements:
- SPM: Minimum 3 credits
- OR equivalent qualifications

SECTION B: PROGRAMME STRUCTURE
--------------------------------
Total Credit Hours: 240 credits

Main Subjects Overview:
- Contemporary Business Environment
- Marketing Processes and Planning
- Leadership and Management
- Global Business Environment
- Employee Relations

Course Structure:

Level 4 Units:
- UNIT 1 – The Contemporary Business Environment
- UNIT 2 – Marketing Processes and Planning
- UNIT 3 – Management of Human Resources
- UNIT 4 – Leadership and Management
- UNIT 5 – Accounting Principles
- UNIT 6 – Managing a Successful Business Project
- UNIT 7 – Business Law
- UNIT 15 – Operational Planning and Management

Level 5 Units:
- UNIT 20 – Organisational Behaviour Management
- UNIT 25 – Global Business Environment
- UNIT 31 – Employee Relations
- UNIT 19 – Research Project
- UNIT 43 – Business Strategy
- UNIT 46 – Developing Individuals, Teams and Organisations
- UNIT 50 – International Marketing

SECTION C: STUDY MODE & ASSESSMENT
--------------------------------
Overall Study Mode:
- Assignment-based (No final exams)

Assessment Requirements:
- Written assignments with proper references and citations
- Submission via Turnitin with plagiarism report
- AI report required

Grading System:
- Pass (P)
- Merit (M)
- Distinction (D)
- Fail
